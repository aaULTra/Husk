- **M8 compatibility:** completed pthread_once uses one acquire load instead of repeatedly attempting a failed sequentially-consistent atomic modification. Initialisation publication and original slow-path behaviour remain. Futex waits retain the original absolute deadline across spurious wakes; address/bitset matching and validation are corrected. Optional included-session counters distinguish initialization, contention, waits, wakes, timeouts and spurious wakes. Completed once calls carry no new collector call. These are concrete code mechanisms, not proof they dominate the game.
- **M4/M6 presentation:** previous default wrote zero desired times into caller-owned memory. Owned bounded copies now retain immediate presentation without those guest writes. New **Clock-Correct Presentation (Experimental)** is default OFF and separately selectable; it maps fresh guest-monotonic/media-clock samples with <=1ms bracket uncertainty and <=50ms scheduling horizon, reverses absolute timing-feedback fields and preserves IDs/duration margins/native errors. No extra queue/wait or independent native-history reader. Known LP64 chain prefixes support at most16 nodes/eight swapchains; unsupported timing prefixes/counts are explicitly rejected, recorded and require rollback if encountered. Invalid-clock data queries defer without draining native history. The current driver can estimate some feedback fields: these are not complete displayed-frame accounting.
- **M9 MetalFX:** validate/cache immutable texture/scaler contracts and set fixed input/content dimensions once per image; retain variable drawable validation/output rebinding and serial encoding. Actual-component Apple tests cover ownership/release, malformed outputs/input, 1000 encodes with single fixed-input setup, concurrent serialization, failure cleanup and reconfiguration after image release. This removes repeated setup; net GPU/CPU energy benefit remains unknown.
- **M1/M3 diagnostics:** schema9 adds bounded compatibility counters and up to256 passive guest presentation-history records, with clock samples, raw native timestamp, calculated mapped time and explicit unavailable/estimated/loss scope. Counters include their stated session boundaries; presentation observations may include lifecycle/excluded periods and cannot establish displayed-frame energy. No per-frame disk output or automatic heavyweight profiling.
- **M9 fixed/dynamic research:** source-backed decision keeps manual budgets and defers automatic scene/whole-image resolution changes until reliable GPU/engine-control/resource-lifetime conditions exist. Temporal MetalFX and dynamic resolution remain unimplemented. No lower default output, hidden quality reduction, speculative driver upgrade or indiscriminate pipeline preparation.

Protected V4, 2128x1600/Sharp Efficient/60, Balanced/Driver Default, thermal safeguards and original-driver/cache restoration remain. Existing worker, engine-only, Async and spatial options remain independent; do not combine their treatments in a controlled comparison.
