## Initial summary-only source audit and interpretation

Collector source and battery arithmetic were re-read against the reported build's implementation. Cadence is included retained interval count divided by summed interval duration, not total wall duration or displayed frames. Percentiles are upper-bin histogram estimates. Thus 55.42 is below the 60 FPS host target for retained included intervals; 16.7 ms median does not prove every section sustained 60 FPS. Whole-session figures cannot distinguish steady gameplay from startup/menus/unmarked interruptions without the JSON windows and exclusions.

The worst interval and 69 slow calls confirm recorded host timing problems requiring investigation. They do not prove that a single 312.79 ms pipeline call caused the 587.229 ms interval. Multiple calls may contribute; other causes and separate non-pipeline stalls remain open. Compare timestamps before assigning cause.

The six native drops can represent contended frame/API/event observation or native event-queue capacity. The aggregate counter does not identify the category. Contention sets a cadence-gap flag, so a later frame can reset the interval anchor; complete coverage is not established even with zero exported-record drops. Their significance must be assessed, not assumed either catastrophic or negligible.

Battery arithmetic returns zero when valid included unplugged spans accumulate no reported decrease. It is not watts, zero electrical consumption, proof of lower drain, or an efficiency improvement. Plateau/coarse or stale API readings and the amount of valid sampling require inspection. Do not classify this as a confirmed battery bug without the JSON. The short session cannot establish small energy differences.

Launch dimensions do not independently prove this run's actual Vulkan swapchain or internal scene dimensions. Selected worker mode does not prove actual QoS application. Check JSON sizes and raw worker/cache logs.

Earlier E-M4-001/E-M4-002 used different workloads, instrumentation and analysis. New collector aggregates retain long hitches that older cadence reporting can discard, and include all unexcluded periods. A direct numerical regression comparison is therefore unsupported. No evidence yet isolates monitoring overhead.
